Point 3 — Control

You stay in control

Human in the loop, always

Aidon presents the plan and consequential live actions for your review, then waits for your approval before acting.

1. Aidon drafts plan
→
2. Presents for review
→
3. Waits for approval
→
4. Acts live

Sending, publishing, buying, installing, and deleting require explicit approval.

How it works

Present → Review → Approval → Act

What Aidon shows you before acting:

  • DAG of steps with dependencies and acceptance criteria
  • Sandbox test results for risky parts
  • Consequential actions flagged for approval
  • Required credentials and external effects

Approval gate

  • Approval requirements depend on the action; privileged operations have their own approval steps
  • Approval is explicit — silence is not consent
  • A rejected or timed-out approval stops the action
Why it matters

Research is cheap, actions are not

Aidon does the heavy lifting in the sandbox, but you stay in control of the real world.

Approval in practice

  • Aidon: "Plan ready — 4 steps, 1 email to send. Approve?"
  • You: Review, edit, or deny
  • Aidon: Executes only after your approval
  • Aidon verifies and records the outcome

You stay in control: review the action and approve it before it goes live.
