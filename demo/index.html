02 · Capability creation, step by step

Earn a place in the toolbox.

The draft stays in the workspace until it passes checks and the owner approves adoption. Once adopted, the capability can be used by later goals.

  1. 01Identify a capability gapCheck existing tools and skills before creating anything new.
  2. 02Draft the tool or skillCreate its interface, manifest, and reusable instructions.
  3. 03Test in isolationTry normal cases, invalid input, and expected failures.
  4. 04Scan and present evidenceShow source findings and test results on the review card.
  5. 05Owner approves adoptionApproval adds the capability to Aidon's available toolbox.

03 · Human control, step by step

Verify first. Ask before acting.

The approval request is tied to the action Aidon plans to take. A rejection ends that path; approval is required before the live action runs.

  1. 01Prepare the requested workBuild a plan with steps, outputs, and clear criteria.
  2. 02Run unproven steps safelyUse the sandbox to check the parts that need proof.
  3. 03Verify the resultCheck outputs against the plan's acceptance criteria.
  4. 04Show the live actionPresent the action and its scope on an approval card.
  5. 05Approve or rejectRejection stops the action; approval allows it to proceed.
  6. 06Execute and check againConfirm the live outcome and recover if it fails.

04 · DAG reuse, step by step

Save the path. Adapt next time.

A DAG makes dependencies and completion criteria explicit. A successful workflow becomes a starting point for similar goals instead of a blank page.

  1. 01Turn the goal into a DAGBreak it into steps, dependencies, deliverables, and criteria.
  2. 02Complete and verify the runCheck the deliverables against the criteria for each step.
  3. 03Save the successful workflowKeep the DAG with the completed run for future recall.
  4. 04Recall it for a similar goalUse prior steps as context when the next goal arrives.
  5. 05Adapt to the new requestAdjust the steps and dependencies to match its differences.
  6. 06Run the updated workflowStart from proven structure, then learn from the new result.
